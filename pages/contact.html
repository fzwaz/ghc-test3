Common Questions

Before you
reach out.

Do you work with early-stage startups? →
Yes. A significant portion of our clients are seed and Series A stage startups. We offer right-sized engagements and understand the resource constraints founders face. See our dedicated startup page for more.
How quickly can you turn around a valuation report? →
Standard turnaround is 3–5 business days for straightforward ESOP or FEMA valuations. Complex M&A fairness opinions typically take 7–10 days. Expedited delivery is available when needed.
Do you work on a retainer or project basis? →
Both. We offer project-based fees for defined deliverables (valuations, transaction mandates) and monthly retainer arrangements for ongoing advisory (Fractional CFO, secretarial, accounting). We'll recommend what suits your situation.
Do you serve clients outside Delhi? →
Absolutely — the majority of our work is conducted remotely. We serve clients across Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, and internationally. Our New Delhi office is our base, but geography is not a barrier.
Is there a minimum engagement size? →
We don't enforce a hard minimum, but we do assess fit before taking on an engagement. If your needs are outside our typical scope, we'll tell you honestly and point you in the right direction.
How confidential is our conversation? →
Completely. All discussions are treated as confidential from the first conversation. We are happy to sign an NDA before any substantive exchange if your situation requires it.
